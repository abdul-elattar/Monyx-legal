This page explains how to delete your Monyx account and associated data.

Delete your account inside the app

  1. Open the Monyx app.
  2. Sign in to your account.
  3. Go to Settings.
  4. Tap Delete account permanently.
  5. Confirm the deletion request.

Once confirmed, your Monyx account will be permanently deleted.

What gets deleted

When you delete your account, the following data associated with your account is intended to be deleted:

  • your authentication account
  • profile information
  • transactions
  • income sources
  • recurring expenses
  • monthly buckets
  • subscriptions
  • goals
  • budget-related records associated with your account

How long deletion takes

Account deletion is intended to happen shortly after you confirm it in the app. In some cases, limited technical records may remain temporarily in backups or logs for security, legal, or operational reasons, after which they are deleted according to normal retention practices.
