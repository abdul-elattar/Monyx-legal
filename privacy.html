Monyx Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Monyx collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you use the Monyx mobile application. Monyx is a personal budgeting and money-planning app designed to help users track income, expenses, subscriptions, savings goals, and monthly budget planning.

1. Who We Are

Monyx is operated by Abdulrahman Elattar. If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, you can contact: abadiraid@gmail.com.

2. Information We Collect

Monyx may collect and store the following information:

  • Account information, such as your email address and authentication details used to sign in.
  • Profile information, such as your selected language, currency, and onboarding preferences.
  • Budgeting and financial planning data, including:
    • income entries and income sources
    • transactions
    • monthly budget items and buckets
    • recurring expenses
    • subscriptions and charge dates
    • savings goals and goal contributions
  • Technical data necessary for the app to function, such as authentication session data stored on your device.

Monyx does not currently request access to sensitive device permissions such as your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or SMS.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used only to provide and improve the core functionality of the app, including:

  • creating and managing your account
  • saving and syncing your budgeting data across sessions
  • displaying your dashboard, goals, subscriptions, and transactions
  • processing recurring monthly budgeting logic
  • maintaining app security and account access
  • responding to support requests

4. How Your Information Is Stored

Monyx uses Supabase as its backend and database provider to store and sync user account and budgeting data. Authentication and app data may be processed and stored on infrastructure used by Supabase.

On your device, certain session-related information may be stored locally so you can remain signed in and continue using the app more conveniently.

5. How We Share Information

Monyx does not sell your personal data.

Your information is only shared when necessary to operate the app, including with:

  • Supabase, for authentication, database storage, and app data synchronization
  • service providers or infrastructure providers, only to the extent necessary to host or operate the service

Monyx does not share your personal budgeting data with advertisers or data brokers.

6. Data Retention

Your data is retained for as long as your account remains active or as needed to provide the app’s services. If you request deletion of your account or your app data, reasonable steps will be taken to delete or anonymize the relevant information, unless retention is required for legal, security, or fraud-prevention reasons.

7. Data Deletion and Your Rights

You may request deletion of your data by contacting: abadiraid@gmail.com.

The app may also provide in-app options to delete synced budgeting data or reset app data. If you want your full account and associated data removed, you may contact the email above and include the email address used for your Monyx account.

8. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to help protect your information. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

9. Children’s Privacy

Monyx is not directed to children under 13, and it is not intended for use by children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through Monyx, please contact abadiraid@gmail.com.

10. International Data Transfers

Depending on your location and the infrastructure used by service providers, your information may be processed in countries other than your own. By using Monyx, you understand that your information may be transferred and processed in this way.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. If material changes are made, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a revised effective date.
